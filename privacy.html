Privacy

Last updated: 31 October 2026. The controller is the provider named in the legal notice.

The Folvane app

Folvane does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The app has no internet connection, no account, no ads, no analytics or crash services.

  • Folvane only reads the folders you choose, and only writes to them when you run or undo a sync or move files to the Recycle Bin. Backups of a sync are kept in a hidden .folvane-backup folder inside that folder; the log stays on your PC only.
  • Settings and the list of recent folder pairs are stored locally on your PC (app data folder) and are removed on uninstall.
  • Reports (PDF, HTML, CSV) are saved only where you choose.

Purchase and installation are handled by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement; we only receive aggregated sales and crash statistics without names.
